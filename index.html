03 · Urgent fixes

Five issues to clear before spending on ads.

Three of these put advertising-platform approval at genuine risk. All five are fixable without touching the site's design.

A hijacked script domain is still loading on every page

Critical

The theme loads code from cdn.polyfill.io - a service taken over in June 2024 and used to serve malicious code, an attack that hit over 100,000 websites. Google responded by blocking Google Ads for e-commerce sites still loading it. A 2016 jQuery build and one unidentified third-party script sit alongside it.

Fix: remove all three. No design impact, no cost beyond the work.

Core ad & analytics tracking is disabled by an old speed app

Critical

Shopify's standard tracking output has been replaced by a script-delaying hack from a page-speed app. Ad pixels and attribution can fire late or not at all - which quietly wastes any money spent on ads.

Fix: restore native loading and remove the app that disabled it.

Most products can't compete properly on Google Shopping

High

404 of 506 active products have no barcode (GTIN), 469 have no SKU. Google reports products with valid GTINs get up to 40% more clicks on Shopping ads. The same data feeds Meta catalogues and AI shopping assistants.

Fix: batch-fill product types and SKUs now; add real GTINs from supplier packaging.

22 prescription products risk the whole ad account

High

Google and Meta prohibit advertising prescription-only medicines. If these reach a shopping feed, the entire Merchant Center account can be suspended - not just those listings.

Fix: exclude them from every ad channel; keep them store-only behind the consultation flow.

72 active products are out of stock

Medium

14% of the live catalogue, including the entire 58-product Colorescience range. Dead product pages lose shoppers and hurt feed standing.

Fix: one decision on Colorescience - restock or unpublish - clears most of this.
