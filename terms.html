1. The service

These terms cover the monthly Store Care and Store Growth plans provided by Quasol Ltd ("we", "us") to the client who subscribes ("you"). Each plan's included work is described on the plan page you subscribed from; anything outside that scope (new pages, redesigns, ad campaign management, one-off projects) is quoted and agreed separately.

2. Billing & minimum term

  • This plan has a minimum term of 12 months from your start date, billed monthly in advance, in GBP, via Stripe.
  • After the initial 12 months, your plan continues automatically on a rolling basis until cancelled.
  • Prices may change with at least 60 days' notice before the change takes effect.

3. Cancellation

You may cancel by giving at least 2 months' (60 days') written notice to hello@quasol.co.uk. Cancellation takes effect at the end of that notice period, and not before your 12-month minimum term has ended. We don't offer refunds for the unused part of a billing period, or for the remainder of the minimum term, unless required by law.

4. What's not guaranteed

We do our best work on your store's health, data quality and marketing setup, but we can't guarantee specific sales, ranking or revenue outcomes - those also depend on your products, pricing, market conditions and decisions outside our plans (such as ad spend). Any figures referenced in our reports are estimates or sourced third-party statistics, not promises.

5. Access & responsibilities

You'll give us the access we reasonably need to your store, apps and marketing tools to do this work. You're responsible for the accuracy of product, pricing and legal information (such as prescription-item status) that you provide to us.

6. Changing plans

You can move between Store Care and Store Growth at any time; the new price applies from your next billing date.
